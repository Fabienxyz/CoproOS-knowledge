# Domain Lifecycle

## Purpose

This document describes the continuous business lifecycle represented by CoproOS.

It explains how the five Core Business Concepts interact over time to create institutional memory and improve governance.

The lifecycle represents the evolution of the condominium, not the execution of software.

---

# Guiding Principle

A condominium never reaches a final state.

It continuously evolves through new commitments, new activities and new knowledge.

The purpose of CoproOS is to preserve this continuity.

---

# Lifecycle

## Step 1 — Observe

Actors observe a situation requiring attention.

Examples include:

- A maintenance issue
- A legal obligation
- A resident request
- A technical recommendation
- A financial opportunity

---

## Step 2 — Commit

One or more Actors create a Commitment.

Examples:

- Conseil Syndical decision
- General Assembly resolution
- Syndic commitment
- Contractor obligation
- Regulatory deadline

A Commitment defines what should happen.

---

## Step 3 — Execute

Activities are performed to fulfil the Commitment.

Execution may involve:

- multiple Actors;
- several Assets;
- multiple Activities over time.

---

## Step 4 — Capture Evidence

Execution generates Evidence.

Examples include:

- Meeting minutes
- Emails
- Quotations
- Contracts
- Reports
- Invoices
- Photographs

Evidence explains what happened.

---

## Step 5 — Build Knowledge

Evidence accumulates into trusted operational knowledge.

Knowledge preserves:

- history;
- context;
- lessons learned;
- operational understanding.

Knowledge survives changes in the Conseil Syndical.

---

## Step 6 — Improve Future Governance

The accumulated Knowledge enables better future Commitments.

The condominium continuously becomes easier to manage because its institutional memory grows over time.

This completes one business cycle and starts the next.

---

# Lifecycle Summary

```text
Observe

↓

Commit

↓

Execute

↓

Capture Evidence

↓

Build Knowledge

↓

Improve Governance

↓

Observe
```

The cycle repeats continuously throughout the life of the condominium.

---

# Design Implications

Every important product capability should support one or more steps of this lifecycle.

The objective of CoproOS is not only to manage the present.

It is to continuously improve future governance through accumulated knowledge.

---

# Governance

The lifecycle should remain stable over time.

Implementation details may evolve.

The business lifecycle should not.
