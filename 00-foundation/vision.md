# Vision

## Purpose

This document defines the long-term vision of CoproOS.

It is the highest-level document in the Knowledge Repository and serves as the strategic reference for every decision made throughout the project.

The Vision explains why CoproOS exists, the future it seeks to create, and the principles that should remain true regardless of how the product evolves.

Every document in the repository should ultimately support this Vision.

---

# Vision Statement

CoproOS exists to professionalize condominium governance by giving volunteer members of the Conseil Syndical the knowledge, continuity and operational capabilities of a permanent professional organization.

By combining institutional memory, structured knowledge and AI-assisted reasoning, CoproOS enables every Conseil Syndical to make better decisions, preserve its collective intelligence and manage its condominium with confidence over decades rather than mandates.

---

# The Future We Want To Create

We believe that the greatest weakness of condominium governance is not a lack of goodwill.

It is the continuous loss of knowledge.

Every election, every resignation and every change of Syndic causes valuable experience, context and decisions to disappear. The same questions are asked again, the same mistakes are repeated and long-term projects lose momentum.

CoproOS changes this.

We envision a future where every condominium possesses a permanent operational memory that survives people, mandates and service providers.

Where every decision is understandable because its history, context and supporting evidence are preserved.

Where volunteer Conseil Syndical members can immediately understand the past, confidently manage the present and prepare the future.

Artificial Intelligence does not replace governance.

It amplifies it by transforming decades of accumulated information into actionable knowledge.

The result is a condominium that becomes progressively easier to manage because its collective intelligence continuously grows.

---

# What CoproOS Will Not Become

CoproOS is intentionally focused.

It is **not**:

- a replacement for the Syndic;
- an accounting system;
- an ERP;
- a project management application;
- a generic document management system;
- a messaging platform;
- a maintenance software.

Those capabilities may integrate with CoproOS when they create value.

They are not its purpose.

CoproOS exists to become the operating system of the Conseil Syndical by preserving knowledge, supporting governance and improving decision-making.

---

# Success Criteria

CoproOS succeeds when:

- a newly elected Conseil Syndical can understand years of history in hours rather than months;
- no important knowledge is ever lost because people leave;
- every commitment remains traceable from creation to completion;
- every important decision is supported by trusted evidence and historical context;
- the relationship with the Syndic becomes more transparent, collaborative and accountable;
- volunteer members spend less time searching for information and more time improving their condominium;
- every year, the condominium becomes easier to govern than the year before.

The ultimate measure of success is not the number of features delivered.

It is the continuous improvement of condominium governance through accumulated institutional knowledge.

---

# Strategic Beliefs

CoproOS is built on a small number of enduring beliefs.

## Governance deserves professional tools.

Volunteer organizations should have access to the same quality of operational support as professional organizations.

---

## Knowledge is the primary asset of a condominium.

Buildings age.

People change.

Knowledge should not disappear.

---

## Institutional Memory creates long-term value.

Every activity, commitment and decision should enrich the collective understanding of the condominium.

---

## AI should augment human judgment.

Artificial Intelligence should help people understand, prioritize and decide.

Responsibility always remains with humans.

---

## Simplicity enables adoption.

The best governance platform is the one that volunteer members continue to use for many years.

Complexity is therefore the enemy of continuity.

---

# Relationship With The Repository

The Vision provides the long-term direction for CoproOS.

The other repository layers progressively translate this vision into an operational product.

- **Foundation** defines the principles and rules that protect the Vision.
- **Product** defines the value delivered to customers.
- **Domain Model** defines the business reality represented by CoproOS.
- **Business** defines the market strategy and economic model.
- **Architecture** defines the technical realization of the product.
- **Decisions (ADR)** record the important choices made throughout the evolution of the project.

Every architectural, product and business decision should remain consistent with this Vision.

When trade-offs are required, the Vision takes precedence.
