# Glossary

## Purpose

This glossary defines the official vocabulary of CoproOS.

Its objective is to ensure that humans and AI systems use the same terminology throughout the project.

Every important business term should have one official definition.

When ambiguity exists, the definitions contained in this document always prevail.

---

# Principles

The glossary should contain:

- stable concepts;
- business terminology;
- architectural concepts when required.

It should avoid:

- implementation details;
- technology-specific terms;
- temporary project vocabulary.

Definitions should be concise, explicit and unambiguous.

---

# Definition Format

Each definition follows the same structure.

## Term

Official name.

## Definition

Single authoritative definition.

## Notes

Optional clarification.

---

# Core Concepts

## Actor

A person or organization interacting with the condominium.

Examples include:

- Board Member
- Board President
- Property Manager
- Contractor
- Supplier
- Co-owner

---

## Asset

A physical or logical object managed by the condominium.

Examples include:

- Building
- Elevator
- Roof
- Boiler
- Contract
- Budget

Assets exist independently from documents.

---

## Activity

A temporary piece of work with a beginning and an end.

Examples include:

- Renovation project
- Insurance claim
- Annual General Meeting
- Contract renewal
- Maintenance campaign

Activities organize work performed over time.

---

## Document

A source of information.

Documents describe reality but are not reality themselves.

Examples include:

- Contract
- Invoice
- Meeting minutes
- Technical report
- Email
- Quotation

---

## Decision

A validated choice made by authorized actors.

A Decision is supported by knowledge, justified by evidence and may generate new Activities.

---

## Knowledge

Validated information that can support decisions.

Knowledge is produced by connecting facts, documents, activities and decisions.

---

## Fact

Verified information supported by evidence.

Facts are objective.

---

## Inference

A conclusion logically derived from one or more facts.

Inferences remain distinguishable from facts.

---

## Hypothesis

A possible explanation or assumption that has not yet been validated.

Hypotheses should never be presented as facts.

---

## Recommendation

A proposed course of action generated by the system.

Recommendations assist decision-making but never replace human responsibility.

---

## Traceability

The ability to identify the origin, history and validation of any important information.

---

## Institutional Memory

The accumulated operational knowledge of the condominium.

Institutional Memory survives changes of Board members and preserves the historical context of decisions.

---

# Governance

Only stable concepts should be defined in this glossary.

Product features, workflows and implementation details belong elsewhere.

When a definition changes, every document using that term should be updated accordingly.
