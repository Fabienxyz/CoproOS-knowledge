# Glossary

## Purpose

This glossary defines the official vocabulary of CoproOS.

Its objective is to ensure that humans and AI systems use the same terminology throughout the project.

Every important business term should have one official definition.

When ambiguity exists, the definitions contained in this document always prevail.

---

# Language Policy

The Knowledge Repository uses **English** as its canonical documentation language.

English maximizes consistency across AI assistants, development tools and future contributors.

However, CoproOS models the legal and operational reality of French condominium governance.

Whenever a French legal or business term has no precise English equivalent, the official French terminology is preserved.

Examples include:

- Conseil Syndical
- Syndic
- Assemblée Générale
- Copropriété

These terms are considered canonical business vocabulary.

Translations may be provided when useful, but the French legal term always takes precedence.

The objective is to preserve both international readability and legal accuracy.

---

# Principles

The glossary should contain:

- stable concepts;
- business terminology;
- architectural concepts when required.

It should avoid:

- implementation details;
- technology-specific terms;
- temporary project vocabulary.

Definitions should be concise, explicit and unambiguous.

---

# Definition Format

Each definition follows the same structure.

## Term

Official name.

## Definition

Single authoritative definition.

## Notes

Optional clarification.

# Core Concepts

These concepts align with the five Core Business Concepts defined in [02-domain-model/](../02-domain-model/).

## Actor

A person or organization interacting with the condominium.

Examples include:

- President of the Conseil Syndical
- Member of the Conseil Syndical
- Syndic
- Contractor
- Supplier
- Co-owner

---

## Asset

A physical or logical object managed by the condominium.

Examples include:

- Building
- Elevator
- Roof
- Boiler
- Contract
- Budget

Assets exist independently from evidence.

---

## Commitment

Something that has been decided, promised, requested or agreed.

Examples include:

- Conseil Syndical decision
- General Assembly resolution
- Syndic commitment
- Contractor obligation
- Regulatory deadline

Commitments may generate Activities.

---

## Activity

A temporary piece of work with a beginning and an end.

Examples include:

- Renovation project
- Insurance claim
- Annual General Meeting
- Contract renewal
- Maintenance campaign

Activities organize work performed over time and exist to fulfil Commitments.

---

## Evidence & Knowledge

**Evidence** is everything that justifies, explains or documents reality.

**Knowledge** is the trusted operational understanding built from evidence over time.

Examples of evidence include:

- Contract
- Invoice
- Meeting minutes
- Technical report
- Email
- Quotation

---

## Fact

Verified information supported by evidence.

Facts are objective.

---

## Inference

A conclusion logically derived from one or more facts.

Inferences remain distinguishable from facts.

---

## Hypothesis

A possible explanation or assumption that has not yet been validated.

Hypotheses should never be presented as facts.

---

## Recommendation

A proposed course of action generated by the system.

Recommendations assist decision-making but never replace human responsibility.

---

## Traceability

The ability to identify the origin, history and validation of any important information.

---

## Institutional Memory

The accumulated operational knowledge of the condominium.

Institutional Memory survives changes of Conseil Syndical members and preserves the historical context of commitments.

---

# Governance

Only stable concepts should be defined in this glossary.

Product features, workflows and implementation details belong elsewhere.

When a definition changes, every document using that term should be updated accordingly.
