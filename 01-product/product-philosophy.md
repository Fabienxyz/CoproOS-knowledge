# Product Philosophy

## Purpose

This document defines the principles that guide product decisions throughout the evolution of CoproOS.

Its objective is to ensure that every feature, enhancement and product decision remains aligned with the Vision and the Strategic Pillars.

---

# Product Vision

CoproOS exists to help volunteer members of the Conseil Syndical manage their condominium with the professionalism, continuity and efficiency of a permanent organization.

Every product decision should reinforce this objective.

---

# Product Principles

## Value Before Features

Customer value always comes before feature quantity.

A smaller product delivering exceptional value is preferable to a larger product with unnecessary complexity.

---

## Solve Real Problems

Every feature should solve a clearly identified problem experienced by the Conseil Syndical.

Ideas without a validated customer problem should not be implemented.

---

## Simplicity Wins

The simplest solution that effectively solves the problem should always be preferred.

Complexity must justify itself.

---

## Knowledge First

Knowledge is the foundation of CoproOS.

Every capability should contribute to creating, preserving, organizing or exploiting trusted knowledge.

---

## AI Is Native

Artificial Intelligence is not a standalone feature.

It is a capability embedded throughout the product wherever it provides meaningful value.

---

## Human Decisions Remain Human

CoproOS assists decision-making.

It never replaces the responsibility of the Conseil Syndical.

Recommendations should always remain explainable and traceable.

---

## Build for Continuity

The product should preserve knowledge beyond the mandate of individual Conseil Syndical members.

Institutional memory is a strategic asset.

---

## Design for Trust

Users should always understand:

- where information comes from;
- why a recommendation is made;
- whether information is verified.

Trust is more valuable than automation.

---

## Evolve Incrementally

The product should evolve through small, validated improvements rather than large speculative redesigns.

---

# Product Decision Framework

Before introducing a new capability, the following questions should always be answered:

1. Which customer problem does it solve?
2. Which Strategic Pillar does it reinforce?
3. Does it simplify or complicate the product?
4. Does it increase user trust?
5. Can its value be explained in less than five minutes?

If one or more answers are unsatisfactory, the capability should be reconsidered.

---

# Governance

This philosophy should remain stable over time.

Individual features may evolve.

The principles guiding product decisions should not.

Every significant product decision should remain consistent with:

- the Vision;
- the Design Principles;
- the Design Rules;
- the Strategic Pillars.
