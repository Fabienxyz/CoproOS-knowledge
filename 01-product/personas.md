# Personas

## Purpose

This document defines the business roles interacting with CoproOS.

It describes their responsibilities, objectives and expectations.

The purpose is to ensure that every product decision is made with a clear understanding of the ecosystem in which CoproOS operates.

---

# Design Principles

Personas represent business roles, not individuals.

They describe long-term responsibilities rather than personal characteristics.

Personas should remain stable over time.

---

# Primary Personas

Primary Personas are the people for whom CoproOS is primarily designed.

## P-01 — President of the Conseil Syndical

### Role

Leads the Conseil Syndical and coordinates its activities.

Acts as the primary sponsor and owner of CoproOS within the condominium.

### Objectives

- Ensure effective governance.
- Coordinate the Conseil Syndical.
- Supervise the Syndic.
- Prepare meetings.
- Preserve continuity.

### Expectations

- Save time.
- Always know what is happening.
- Make informed decisions.
- Access historical knowledge instantly.

---

## P-02 — Member of the Conseil Syndical

### Role

Participates in the governance of the condominium.

Contributes to decisions and follows assigned responsibilities.

### Objectives

- Stay informed.
- Follow ongoing activities.
- Prepare decisions.
- Contribute efficiently.

### Expectations

- Quickly understand situations.
- Access trusted information.
- Reduce administrative workload.

---

# Secondary Personas

Secondary Personas interact regularly with the Conseil Syndical and directly benefit from CoproOS.

## P-03 — Syndic

### Role

Manages the operational and administrative responsibilities of the condominium.

Collaborates continuously with the Conseil Syndical.

### Objectives

- Execute agreed actions.
- Share information.
- Coordinate operations.

### Expectations

- Clear communication.
- Transparent follow-up.
- Better meeting preparation.

---

## P-04 — Co-owner

### Role

Owns one or more units within the condominium.

Receives information and participates in General Meetings.

### Objectives

- Understand what happens in the condominium.
- Trust the Conseil Syndical.
- Access reliable information.

### Expectations

- Better communication.
- Greater transparency.
- Faster answers.

---

# Ecosystem Personas

These personas are not primary users of CoproOS but are important actors in the condominium ecosystem.

## P-05 — Contractor

### Examples

- Electrician
- Plumber
- Roofing company
- Painter
- Cleaning company
- Construction company

### Role

Executes operational work requested by the condominium.

---

## P-06 — Supplier

### Examples

- Utility provider
- Insurance company
- Software provider
- Maintenance provider

### Role

Provides recurring products or services to the condominium.

---

## P-07 — Expert

### Examples

- Architect
- Structural engineer
- Energy consultant
- Surveyor
- Legal expert

### Role

Provides specialized expertise supporting important decisions.

---

## P-08 — Public Authority

### Examples

- Municipality
- Urban planning department
- Public administration
- Regulatory authority

### Role

Interacts with the condominium whenever legal or administrative processes require it.

---

# Governance

Product decisions should primarily optimize the experience of the Primary Personas.

Secondary Personas should benefit from the product without driving its design.

Ecosystem Personas provide context for the Domain Model and future product capabilities.

Whenever trade-offs are necessary, the needs of the Conseil Syndical take precedence.
