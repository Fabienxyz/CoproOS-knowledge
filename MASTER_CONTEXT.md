# MASTER_CONTEXT

## Purpose

This document is the primary entry point for AI assistants and new contributors.

Its purpose is to provide minimal context and direct readers toward the canonical documents of the Knowledge Repository.

It intentionally contains summaries only.

Detailed knowledge must remain in its authoritative documents.

# Project

**CoproOS** is an AI-native operating system for the Conseil Syndical.

Its mission is to professionalize condominium governance by preserving institutional memory, improving decision-making and enabling long-term continuity.
